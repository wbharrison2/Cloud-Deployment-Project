About Artisan Gem Works

Founded in 2019 by Mira Chen, Artisan Gem Works began as a craft-fair table in Portland's Pearl District. After five years and a loyal following across the Pacific Northwest, we opened our second home in Seattle's Ballard neighborhood in 2024.

Every piece is still shaped, soldered, and finished by hand — carrying the spirit of the landscape that inspired it.
